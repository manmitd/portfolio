Hi, I'm Manmit

I'm a product designer based in Vancouver with 5+ years of experience across web and mobile. I'm currently the Lead Product Designer at Sewjo, where I've shaped the product since its early days across consumer experiences, marketplace features, and business tools. Earlier this year, I led design on Wildlight, an AI‑powered product built with Accenture and showcased at Web Summit Vancouver 2026.

I came to design with a background in law, and I think it shows. I like messy systems full of rules and edge cases, and I like making them feel simple for the person on the other side.
