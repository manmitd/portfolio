Hi, I'm Manmit

I'm a product designer based in Vancouver with 5+ years of experience, currently leading design at Sewjo, where I've been helping build the product from its early days.

My work has taken me across consumer apps, marketplaces, and AI‑powered experiences. I've worked on everything from shaping product strategy and untangling complex workflows to prototyping ideas and bringing them into production.
